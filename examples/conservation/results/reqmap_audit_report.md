# Item 0 — §2.7 requirement-map audit report

## Question
Do all 21 cases in the §2.7 two-axis convergence matrix run under a single
requirement map, or does the matrix mix objects?

## Method
For each case in `_all_cases()`, compare `case.fine_reqs` to
`_default_fine_reqs()`. Tabulate every (case, level, gap) deviation.
Classify deviations as AAA-only (not load-bearing — capped by authority
ceiling) or chain-level (potentially load-bearing — touches DIA/REV/AEX/ALR).

## Finding

| Family | Cases | Clean | Deviating |
|---|---|---|---|
| Induction (M01–M07) | 7 | 7 | 0 |
| Held-out (H01–H05) | 5 | 5 | 0 |
| Synthetic ladder | 5 | 4 | 1 (S-REF) |
| Active witnesses | 4 | 0 | 4 (W-currency, W-deployment, W-population, W-clinical) |

**Verdict: the §2.7 matrix mixes maps in a load-bearing way.**

Specifically:

1. **S-REF runs under a one-off custom map** with strict `closed` on
   approximation_quality_gap at every level (REV, AEX, ALR, AAA) and
   no other gap requirements at AEX/ALR/AAA. This is a 13-diff deviation
   from canonical, touching DIA, REV, AEX and ALR. S-REF's emit (REF) is
   *generated by* this custom map; the canonical map cannot produce REF.

2. **Four active-refinement witnesses each upgrade exactly one ALR gap
   from `bounded` (canonical) to `closed` (strict)**:
   - W-currency: freshness_gap closed at ALR
   - W-deployment: blast_radius_gap closed at ALR
   - W-population: distribution_shift_gap closed at ALR
   - W-clinical: clinical_utility_gap closed at ALR

   These deviations are by construction. The witnesses exist *because*
   the strict-closed requirement is what gets poisoned by the merged
   composite at L4 and resolved by the split. Without the deviation, the
   witnesses don't witness anything.

## Implications for §2.7

### §2.7.2 main resolving-path table
- Runs across all 21 cases, four levels of refinement, five permission
  grids = 525 cells.
- Every cell is *within-case*: A(e) for case c at level m, grid k. Each
  case's A(e) is computed under that case's own `fine_reqs`.
- The aggregate stat "525/525 monotone" is a *per-case-under-its-own-map*
  claim, not a cross-case uniformity claim. It is true and provable in
  that form, but the v3 text presents it as if all cases share a map.

### §2.7.3 active-refinement witnesses
- The four witnesses are deliberately under strict-closed maps. The §2.7.3
  claim "each witness rises AEX → ALR at its target split" is *within
  each witness*. This is correct as written but should explicitly note
  that the witnesses are constructed under non-canonical maps.

### §2.7.4 non-resolving path
- 10 unsound cells: H02 (5) + S-REV (5), both canonical-map. Honest as
  reported.
- 80 monotonicity-break cells at L5→L4 across 16 cases. The 16 include
  the 4 witnesses (with strict-closed maps) and 12 canonical-map cases.
  The break pattern is structurally the same in both cases (skeleton
  truncation at L5 inflates emit; admissible projection at L4 restores
  the strict requirement and drops the emit), but the underlying maps
  differ.

### Aggregate "21/21 jointly converged"
- Each case converges to its own A(e) at (m=0, k=64) under its own map.
  S-REF converges to REF under the strict-AQ map; witnesses converge to
  ALR under their strict-closed maps; the 16 canonical-map cases converge
  to AEX/REV/DIA/ALR under canonical. The aggregate is honest in the
  per-case-under-own-map sense.

## Required v3 text changes

The v3 text overstates uniformity. The minimum revision:

1. **Add a one-paragraph caveat to §2.7.1** explicitly stating that the
   matrix evaluates each case under its own requirement map. The active
   witnesses are deliberately under strict-closed maps to create resolvable
   active blockers. S-REF is deliberately under a strict-AQ map to produce
   REF emit. All other 16 cases run under the canonical map.

2. **Reframe aggregate stats as per-case-under-own-map**, e.g.:
   - "525 cells (= 21 cases × 5 levels × 5 grids); within each case under
     its own requirement map, every cell is sound and monotone."
   - "21/21 cases joint-converge to their case's A(e)."

3. **Add a footnote or short subsection** acknowledging that the §2.7.2
   aggregate is not a cross-case uniformity claim. Different requirement
   maps produce different A(e); the aggregate says the framework's
   per-case behavior is consistent in form across maps, not that the
   numerical convergence target is shared.

4. **Optional but recommended**: report the 16-case canonical-map subset
   separately as the "pure" main result, then report the witnesses and
   S-REF as supplementary tests under modified maps. This is the cleanest
   presentation.

## What this does NOT undermine

The substantive §2.7 results survive the audit intact:
- Joint convergence at (m=0, k=64) holds for every case under that case's
  map. The within-case theorem is valid.
- The L5 skeleton-truncating projection produces unsound emits in the two
  canonical-map cases where A(e) < AEX (H02, S-REV). The admissibility
  violation is real.
- The L5→L4 monotonicity break is real and localized to the
  inadmissibility-restoration transition for both canonical and witness
  maps.
- Active-refinement witnesses fire at their target splits under their
  own maps. The chain-bridge duality result holds.

## What this audit changes

The change is rhetorical, not substantive. The matrix is honest if
described as "21 cases each under its own map", and dishonest if described
as "21 cases under one map". The current v3 text is closer to the second
formulation than the first.
